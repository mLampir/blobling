# Blobling – Privacy Policy

*[Deutsche Fassung](privacy.de.md)*

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy covers the Blobling app for Android and iOS and the pet page of
a Blobling LED display at home.

**In short:** Blobling has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and
no in-app purchases. There is no Blobling server: your pet lives on your
phone, or on your own LED display at home. The only thing that ever leaves
your device is a rounded location, and only if you turn on the weather.

## 2. What the app stores on your phone

- **Your pet**: its name, needs, form, finds and diary.
- **Settings**: the language, whether the weather is on, the location
  (rounded, see section 3) and the address of your LED display, if you
  connected one.

All of this stays in the app's storage on your phone. None of it is sent to
us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

**Notifications**: the app asks whether it may show notifications. If you
allow it, the pet on your phone calls with one when it needs you (never at
night). The app sets it up on the phone itself when you leave it, and the
phone's own system shows it at that time; nothing is sent anywhere for it,
and there is no push service. You turn notifications off in the phone's
settings.

## 3. Location and weather

The weather in the pet's world is optional and off until you turn it on.
When you do, the app asks your phone for its location (only while the app is
open, through the system's permission dialog), either once or each time it
is opened, as you choose. The position is rounded to two decimals, about
1 km, and only this rounded position is stored and used. *Forget the
location* in the app removes it.

To show the weather, the app sends the rounded position to
[Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) (`api.open-meteo.com`) over an encrypted
connection, about every 15 minutes while it is open, and Open-Meteo returns
the current weather. No name, account or device ID is sent with it; like any
request on the internet, it carries your IP address. Open-Meteo's own terms
and privacy policy apply to its servers: <https://open-meteo.com/en/terms>.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You withdraw it at any
time by letting the app forget the location or by taking the location
permission away in the phone's settings.

## 4. Device backups (Google backup on Android, iCloud backup on iOS)

If the system backup is turned on on your phone, the operating system also
backs up the app's storage, so your pet comes along to a new phone or a new
installation. The backup is made by Google (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon
House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) as part of your Google account, or
by Apple as part of your Apple Account; we have no access to it. It holds
what section 2 lists.

- **Android**: the backup is kept in your Google account, or carried directly
  from phone to phone when you switch. You turn it off in the Android
  settings (depending on the maker under *Google → Backup*).
- **iOS**: the backup is kept in your iCloud. You turn it off, or leave
  Blobling out of it, in the iOS settings under your name → *iCloud*.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in letting you
keep your pet when you change phones. Whether and where anything is backed up
is up to you in the phone's settings. Privacy:
<https://policies.google.com/privacy>, <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>

## 5. Your own LED display

If you connect the app to a Blobling display (a Raspberry Pi) at home, the
app talks to it directly over your home network, and the pet lives on the
display. The display is your own device; nothing goes through us. The
display itself asks Open-Meteo for the weather at the place you set on it,
and GitHub for new versions of its software.
