## App Store: notes for App Review

(App Store Connect, the version's *App Review Information → Notes*; the same
text fits Google Play's *App access*, with "All functionality is available
without special access".) Reviewers write in English, so this exists in
English only.

Blobling needs no account, no login and no hardware: the whole game runs on
the phone. A new pet starts as an egg that hatches within about an hour
(stroking it keeps it warm and makes it hatch sooner). Then drag food from the
tray onto the pet and stroke it. A tap on the pet's name renames it or starts
a new egg; the "More" tab has sound and the optional weather.

The section "Own LED display" in "More" is optional. It connects the app to
a self-built LED panel (a Raspberry Pi on the home network, instructions at
https://github.com/mLampir/blobling). That is why the app asks for local
network access and talks plain HTTP to private addresses only. Without such
a display, the search finds nothing, asks for an address, and the pet stays
on the phone.

Location is optional and only used for the weather in the pet's world: the
app rounds it to about 1 km and sends it to Open-Meteo. Notifications are
local only (the pet asks for you when it needs you); there is no server.

## Description (Google Play full description and App Store description)

Blobling is a little pixel pet that lives on your phone. It hatches from an egg, wants you a few times a day and grows up with you. In time come new eggs, new kinds of pets and a whole street of them.

IT FEELS EVERY TOUCH
Drag a meal or the ball onto your pet, scrub it with the sponge, stroke it and tuck it in at night. It answers right where it is, with a happy face, a soft buzz and a chiptune sound.

IT LIVES ON YOUR TIME
The sky follows your real time of day, with sunrise, sunset and tonight's moon. Turn on the weather, and the rain and snow outside fall in its world too. On a cold morning it wakes up shivering until you rub it warm, and on a hot afternoon it wants a bath. While you are away, it plays, builds little things and practices on its own, and when you come back it tells you about its day.

WHAT WILL IT BECOME?
Your baby grows into a child, and the child into a grown-up. Which form it takes depends on what it did as a child, and sometimes on the right moment, like a night of full moon. The final choice is yours. Secret forms leave only trails to follow: a story a friend tells, or an old note by the path.

TRIPS, TRICKS AND TRAINING
Teach it tricks with a swipe, train its strength, speed and smarts on its own training ground, and send it out: the meadow, the mushroom forest, the stream, a cave, the mountain, and beyond them the coast with real tides and an old castle in ruins. It comes home with seeds, hats, decorations and gear for the training ground, and with the scuffles it had on the way, which you can watch again. Each area has a guardian to get past.

MORE THAN 25 KINDS OF PETS
Every egg can be something new. Eggs come from guardians, from wild pets your pet wins over with its tricks, from guests that turn up at the garden fence, from the pond, from shooting stars, from the trick festival every other Saturday, from a treasure map and from Grandma's attic. A friend's egg takes after the friend, the more the longer they have been friends. Every form your pets have been fills a page of the album, and a line you have raised in full leaves a keepsake.

IT WON'T STAY ALONE FOR LONG
Up to four pets live next door to each other, each in a house of its own. They meet on the playground for the swing, the sandpit and the seesaw, copy each other's tricks and share a yard with a trough and a water basin they visit by themselves. On dry evenings they all sit around the campfire before bed. A pet that makes room for a new egg moves into the album and can come home again whenever you like.

GENTLE BY DESIGN
Your pet never dies, and nothing is lost for good. Forget it for a day and a half and it goes to Grandma's, where time stands still for it until you fetch it. Going on holiday? Take it there yourself, and it may come home with a knitted hat.

A DIARY OF ITS LIFE
Its history, every trip, every find, its stats and the album are kept in its diary.

ON AN LED DISPLAY, TOO (OPTIONAL)
Your pets can also live on a small LED panel at home, driven by a Raspberry Pi. The app connects to it on your home network, the pets there go on with their day while your phone is off, and you can take one along on your phone.

PRIVATE
No account, no ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases. Your pets live on your phone. The only thing that ever leaves it is a rounded location for the weather, and only if you turn the weather on.

Blobling is open source: the app and the LED display, with build instructions, are on GitHub (mLampir/blobling).

In English and German.
